Cape Control

The heroes save the city. You tell them where.

Port Ferris has more superhumans than any city in the country, and somebody has to answer the phone. You're the night dispatcher of a small hero agency: calls light up the city map, you pick who goes, and you talk them through it on the radio.

Thirty heroes, each with a life of their own. Sign them, pay them, keep their spirits up in the break room, and hold on to them when the big agency comes calling. A gang that phones in decoys, a sponsor with ideas, a mole somewhere on your roster, and City Hall's review at the end of every season.
